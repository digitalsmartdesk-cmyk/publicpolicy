The Household Balance Sheet tracker

Household financial liabilities rose from 36.4% to 45.9% of GDP in fifteen quarters, June 2022 to March 2026 — almost twice the growth rate of the economy itself. Nine indicators, each with a value, a trend and a peer anchor.

The seat-allocation calculator

Set a House size and a rule — Freeze, Population, a Mixture, or Add-and-Protect — and the calculator gives every state's seats, the change from today, and the change in tax share, built on 2011 Census population. It is a way to check the arithmetic, not an argument.
