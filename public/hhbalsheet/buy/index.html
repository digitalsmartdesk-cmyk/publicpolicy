The Household Balance Sheet

What Indian Families Own, Owe and Earn

Household debt grew 78% in under four years; the economy it sits on grew 41%. Household financial liabilities rose from 36.4% to 45.9% of GDP between June 2022 and March 2026. Fifteen chapters track where the borrowing went — gold loans, small fintech loans, cards — and who is most exposed if the music stops.
