About & Method

Better policy starts with credible evidence

Public Policy Foundation is an independent research group on India's economic and social development. We are not a registered entity: there is no donate button, no grants or CSR page, and no membership. We earn our keep by selling research — books, data and commissioned studies — to readers, professionals and institutions.

Independence statement

Clients choose the question, not the conclusion.

Commissioned work follows the same sourcing standard as our books. We agree the scope and the sources before work begins; we do not agree the finding in advance, and we do not offer retainers, advocacy or lobbying services.

Our mission

To undertake and disseminate rigorous policy research that generates actionable insights, informs public decision-making, and contributes to inclusive, sustainable and equitable development.

Economic Development

Growth, employment, industry, trade, agriculture, infrastructure, public finance and small business — the policies and conditions that shape economic opportunity.

Social Development

Education, health, poverty and inequality, gender, housing and essential services — the public systems that shape wellbeing and quality of life.

How our data is built

Every figure is tagged by how sure we are of it

Each chart and table carries a source, a date, and one of six tiers:

Primary

Read directly from the original release.

Calculated

Derived from primary figures by a stated formula.

Compiled

Assembled from more than one primary source.

Press report

From news coverage of an unpublished release; flagged to verify.

Illustrative

A worked example, not a measured value.

To source

Named as missing, not filled with a guess.

Where two sources disagree, our books show both. Corrections to published figures are listed on each book's own corrections page, dated. Charts and tracker data carry the date they were last checked.
